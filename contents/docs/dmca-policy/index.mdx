## Siputra Digital

Last Updated: May 7, 2026

Siputra Digital respects intellectual property rights and expects all clients to do the same.

This DMCA Policy outlines the process for reporting copyright infringement or unauthorized use of copyrighted materials on websites developed or managed by Siputra Digital.

## 1. Copyright Responsibility

Clients are fully responsible for all content published on their websites, including but not limited to:

- Text
- Images
- Videos
- Logos
- Graphics
- Downloads
- Articles
- Product listings
- Other uploaded materials

Clients must ensure they have the legal rights, licenses, or permissions to use all submitted content.

Siputra Digital does not verify ownership of materials provided by clients and assumes no liability for copyright violations caused by client content.

## 2. Reporting Copyright Infringement

If you believe that copyrighted material has been used improperly on a website developed or managed by Siputra Digital, you may submit a copyright infringement notice.

Your notice should include:

- Your full name and contact information;
- A description of the copyrighted work;
- The URL or location of the allegedly infringing content;
- Evidence or proof of ownership;
- A statement confirming that the information provided is accurate.

Incomplete or misleading reports may not be processed.

## 3. Investigation Process

Upon receiving a valid copyright complaint, Siputra Digital may:

- Review the reported content;
- Contact the website owner or client;
- Request clarification or supporting documents;
- Temporarily disable access to disputed content if necessary.

We reserve the right to determine appropriate actions based on the information provided.

## 4. Repeat Violations

Siputra Digital reserves the right to suspend or terminate services for clients repeatedly involved in copyright infringement or unlawful content distribution.

## 5. Limitation of Liability

Siputra Digital acts solely as a website development service provider.

We are not responsible for:

- Content uploaded by clients;
- Copyright violations committed by website owners;
- Legal disputes between third parties and clients.

All legal responsibility for website content remains entirely with the Client.

## 6. False Claims

Submitting false, misleading, or fraudulent copyright complaints may result in legal consequences.

Complainants are responsible for the accuracy and legitimacy of submitted claims.

## 7. Policy Updates

Siputra Digital reserves the right to modify or update this DMCA Policy at any time without prior notice.

The latest version will always be available on this page.
